# English (en)
- Natural US English. Sentence case for headings and buttons.
- Characters: the Doctor, the Assistant (Haru; stage name Sena), the Fruit Fly Researcher, AbaBot (see glossary).
- The Doctor explains warmly; verdicts are short declaratives ("AbaBot wins.").
- Fruit Fly Researcher: short, skeptical, casual. AbaBot: casual, investor slang ("I'm all in", "cut my losses", "break even").
- Use "you" for the reader. Avoid stiff literal phrasing of Korean "~해요".
